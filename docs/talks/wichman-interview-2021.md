Glenn Wichman, who wrote the first Rogue with Michael Toy at UC Santa Cruz, talks to
Taigen Moon of the YouTube channel The Cellar: how he and Toy met in the computer lab in 1978,
curses and the ADM-3A terminals, the players in the stat lab, permadeath and mystery items, the
three stages of Rogue's making (Santa Cruz, Berkeley with Ken Arnold, and A.I. Design with
Jon Lane), drawing the Macintosh graphics and writing the Atari ST version for Epyx, and what he
would do differently.

See also the [2016 panel with Michael Toy and Ken Arnold](rogue-panel-2016.md).
