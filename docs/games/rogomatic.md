# Rog-O-Matic XIV

The classic Rogue-playing program developed at **Carnegie Mellon
University** by Andrew Appel, Leonard Hamey, Guy Jacobson, and Michael
Mauldin. Rog-O-Matic was, for a time, the strongest known Rogue player —
human or machine — and it remains one of the earliest substantial pieces
of game-playing AI to survive in source form.
